Help · for after you have downloaded Zelos

Set up Zelos with Claude's help.

Every setup step below has a message already written for you. Hand it to Claude and Claude will walk you through that one step — one thing at a time, in plain words, asking you what you see. Nothing about you is in the message: not your email address, not a password. Only which screen you are on and what it needs.

How it works

  1. Find the step you are stuck on below and press Copy this message.
  2. Press Ask Claude to walk me through this. Claude opens in a new tab. If you have never used Claude, it asks you to sign in or make a free account first.
  3. If the message is already sitting in the chat box, press send. If the box is empty, paste the message in — Cmd+V on a Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows — and press send.

Claude will then ask you what you see on your screen. Answer in your own words. Never paste a password, an app password, a key or a sign-in code into the chat. Claude does not need them, and the message tells it so. Prefer ChatGPT? Every card has an or ChatGPT link that does the same thing.
