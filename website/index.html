How it works

Your context.
AI that can use it.

Zelos is the workspace around the AI. It brings in records, finds relevant context, gives that context to your chosen model, and puts useful results where you can review them.

  1. 01

    Connect what matters.

    Add the mail accounts, calendars, project sources, and local files you want Zelos to read. It keeps an archive on your computer. You choose the sources; Zelos checks them while it is open.

  2. 02

    Give AI the relevant background.

    For a question or check, Zelos gathers context from your records and asks the model you configured to interpret it. Instead of manually copying a thread into a chat, you can ask in the place that already holds the context.

  3. 03

    Turn the answer into a next step.

    See priorities, review meeting prep, follow source references, or inspect an assigned task’s result. Edit a draft, correct a detail, snooze an item, or mark it done. AI helps prepare the work; you decide what to trust and act on.

Your mail, calendar & notes → Zelos finds the context → Your chosen AI → A result to review

Your AI, on your terms

Choose the model.
Keep the workspace.

Zelos does not lock your records to one AI provider. Configure a compatible online model, or run one locally, and keep working in the same place.

An online model

Use a compatible provider with your own API key and configuration. Zelos sends the context needed for a request to that provider.

This gives you access to hosted models without running one on your own hardware. Provider charges, capabilities, and data policies apply.

A model on your computer

Use a compatible local model endpoint, such as a model served through Ollama or LM Studio. Model processing can stay on your machine.

This gives you more control over where context is processed. Speed and answer quality depend on the model and your hardware; connected sources still contact their own services.

What the AI does—and what you still control.

The model helps interpret, summarize, prioritize, and prepare work from the context Zelos provides. It can miss details or get an answer wrong. Check source references and review results before relying on them. The workspace also gives you direct search, calendars, records, and task controls that do not need an AI answer for every click.

In the current build, email sending requires review and your explicit action. Imported records are reviewed before saving. Grocery checkout takes place with the store. Other AI clients can receive scoped, read-only access only when you enable and configure that sharing.

A few good questions

Know what
you’re bringing home.

How is Zelos different from opening a chat with an AI? +

Zelos combines a local archive, connected sources, and everyday views with an AI model you choose. You can ask about saved records without rebuilding all the background in each conversation, then keep working with tasks, calendars, drafts, and plans in the same workspace.

The AI model supplies language and reasoning capabilities. Zelos supplies the context, the workflow, and the controls around the result.

Does Zelos include an AI subscription? +

No. Zelos is free and open source. For an online model, you configure a compatible provider and may pay that provider for API usage. A chat subscription does not necessarily include API usage.

You can instead configure a local model. It needs suitable hardware and a compatible local endpoint. The website demo uses prepared examples and has no AI usage charge.

Does it automatically do things on my behalf? +

You can assign supported work, such as researching saved records, preparing an unsent reply, saving a note, or creating a weekly report. Zelos records the steps so you can inspect the result.

Sending email is a reviewed action in the current build. It does not silently complete a grocery checkout. Booking publication and external connections require their own setup.

Where do my records and AI requests go? +

The archive lives on your computer. Connected sources contact their own services. When you choose an online AI, the relevant content for the request goes to that provider.

A local model can keep model processing on your machine. Read the privacy page for the public release and the preview’s additional connection requirements.

Can I use the new interface shown in the demo today? +

The website previews the current development interface with fictional records. The public installer below is version {{VERSION}}, with an earlier interface and a smaller feature set.

The demo lets you explore without connecting real accounts. Changes reset on reload, and its prepared answers illustrate the experience rather than running a live model.
